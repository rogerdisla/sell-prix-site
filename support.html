Frequently asked questions

La Ficha help, made simple.

Choose a question to see an answer. For individual help, email support@sellprixllc.com.

How do I keep score?

Choose a points-to-win target, then add points to Team 1 or Team 2 after each round. Quick-add and manual score controls make adjustments easier.

How do I count dominoes using the camera?

Choose Take Photo, frame all the dominoes, and capture a sharp image. Review the detected total before adding it to the correct team. Good lighting and a steady phone help accuracy.

What if the camera misses a pip?

You can change the detected total manually before assigning it to a team. If the photo is blurry, try again with the entire domino face visible.

Why does La Ficha request camera access?

Camera permission lets you take pictures for domino pip counting. Your device settings let you review or change that permission. You can also choose a photo from your gallery.

Can I use La Ficha in Spanish?

La Ficha includes English and Spanish interface options. You can switch between them using the language controls.
