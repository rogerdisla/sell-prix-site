Good to know

Questions about La Ficha?

A few answers before your next game. Visit our support FAQs for more details.

How does photo counting work?

Take a clear picture of the dominoes, review the detected pip total, and choose which team receives the points. Keep all tiles visible and the camera steady for best results.

Can I correct a detected score?

Yes. You can adjust the detected total manually before adding it to a team.

Is La Ficha available in the App Store?

Not yet. La Ficha is in development; official availability and download information will be posted here when confirmed.
