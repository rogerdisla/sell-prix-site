1. About La Ficha

La Ficha is a dominoes scorekeeping app developed by SELL PRIX LLC. It includes team scoring, camera-assisted pip counting, and interface language options. [VERIFY the features and platforms in the final submitted build.]

2. Photos and camera permissions

La Ficha provides camera-assisted counting of domino tiles and allows users to choose an image for analysis. The reviewed iPhone web implementation decodes selected photos on the device and analyzes their image pixels locally using the browser's canvas tools. It does not require sending domino photographs to SELL PRIX LLC to count pips. Users can also enter scores manually.

The reviewed image-processing workflow uses the selected photo in memory and on a local preview canvas. No photo transmission or server-side recognition code was identified in the examined web build.

Verify whether the app saves images to its local files, Photos library, or temporary caches, how such content is erased, and that photo files are not transferred to advertising or analytics providers. The analyzed HTML version was not the final native build.

3. Game scores and preferences

La Ficha keeps track of team scores, round history, the selected target score, team names, and interface language preferences. In the reviewed iPhone web implementation, these settings are saved in the device's browser local storage, using the key la-ficha-state-v1851. No corresponding cloud synchronization or remote scoring endpoint was identified in that examined build.

[VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION] Confirm final native-app storage, iOS backup behavior, whether any analytics SDK receives game-state data, and how users can clear saved scores and team names. Local storage can persist between sessions and may be erased when app/site data is cleared; do not promise automatic deletion until verified.

4. Advertising and third-party services

SELL PRIX LLC plans to include live Google AdMob banner advertising in the first iOS release of La Ficha. Banner advertisements will be served using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and participating advertising partners may process information including IP addresses (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and app interactions, depending on the final SDK setup, available permissions, user choices, and ad serving configuration.

Advertising data may be used for displaying advertisements, measuring their performance, analytics, fraud prevention, and—only when permitted—personalization. Ad-related information is processed by Google and potentially advertising partners. For details on Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosure guidance.

[VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION] Confirm the Google Mobile Ads SDK is included in the signed iOS release build; enumerate all mediation, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs actually present; verify the exact data categories, purposes, processing roles, linked-data treatment, and tracking status. 5. Consent and privacy controls

Users can manage camera permission in device settings. To address advertising privacy choices, the planned iOS integration will use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to present regulatory consent choices, including the ability to revisit those choices using a visible in-app privacy settings option when required.

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization must be requested if the final app performs cross-app or cross-website tracking within Apple's definition. Declining tracking permission does not itself prevent all advertising or other non-tracking data processing; applicable advertising and privacy settings will control what is permitted.
