La Ficha · App privacy

Privacy Policy

How SELL PRIX LLC handles information when you use La Ficha, including domino photos, locally saved game scores, and advertising.

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Publisher: SELL PRIX LLC

La Ficha is a dominoes scorekeeping application developed by SELL PRIX LLC. This policy describes the currently designed app functionality and the advertising features planned for the first iOS release. The iOS app is still in development. We will review and, if necessary, update this policy to reflect the final released app and its integrated services before launch.

1. Information processed to keep score

La Ficha lets you record team scores, round history, a winning-score target, team names, and language preferences. In the current web-based implementation, that information is saved locally on your device using browser storage. We do not operate accounts or a cloud synchronization service for this game data in that implementation.

The local storage can persist across app sessions. If you clear game history using available app controls, that history is cleared as designed; clearing the app's or browser's stored data may remove other locally saved settings as well. The behavior of device backups and app deletion depends on your device and platform.

2. Domino photos and camera access

La Ficha offers camera-assisted domino pip counting. You can take a photo or choose an existing image to calculate a suggested score, which you can review and correct. The currently reviewed web implementation analyzes the image on your device using local image-processing tools. Its pip-counting function does not require uploading the photograph to a SELL PRIX LLC server.

Images may be held temporarily in memory or a local preview while counting. We do not use your domino photographs for advertising. The camera is accessed only when you use a feature that needs it, subject to your device's permissions. You can manage camera permission in your device settings and use manual scoring instead.

3. Advertising, identifiers and technical information

La Ficha's first iOS release is planned to contain Google AdMob banner advertising through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and applicable advertising partners may collect or process technical information such as your device's IP address (which may indicate general location), device or app identifiers, advertising impressions and interactions, app interactions, performance details, and diagnostic or crash information, depending on their services, your settings, and applicable law.

Advertising information may be used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, analyze performance, and provide personalized advertising where permitted. Some information may be linked to a device or identifier. We are not planning to install separate Firebase Analytics, standalone crash-reporting, or additional ad-network mediation SDKs in the initial iOS version; the Google Mobile Ads SDK itself may still collect analytics and diagnostics information.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for more about Google's processing. The providers and data uses available to users may vary depending on applicable advertising settings and regional consent requirements.

4. Consent and privacy controls

Where applicable, La Ficha's planned iOS advertising integration will use Google's User Messaging Platform to present choices about advertising-related data processing. You may be offered options to consent, decline, or manage specific purposes. Some advertising partners may rely on other legal bases where the law allows, and applicable objection rights will be provided through the consent interface.

When the app is released, we intend to provide access to advertising privacy choices from the app's settings wherever a privacy options control is required. If Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission applies to the final advertising configuration, we will request permission before performing tracking that requires it. You can also review applicable permissions in iOS Settings. Declining optional advertising permissions will not prevent basic domino scorekeeping.

For questions or assistance with privacy choices, contact support@sellprixllc.com.

5. Sharing of information

We do not intend to send domino photos, team names, or game scores to Google for advertising. The locally processed game information described above is separate from the technical and advertising data that Google or its authorized partners may process to operate and measure ads. Advertising partners are responsible for their processing under their applicable policies and legal obligations.

We may also disclose information received directly from you, such as support emails, when needed to respond to requests, meet legal obligations, protect security, or operate our business using appropriate service providers.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally saved scores and preferences may remain on your device until you clear the relevant data or remove the app, subject to any device backup and restoration mechanisms. We do not maintain a separate cloud account containing those game scores in the current implementation.

If you email us, we may retain your message and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary for support, business records, security, or legal obligations. Google and its advertising partners may retain technical or advertising data according to their own policies and applicable law. You may contact us to request assistance concerning information we control.

7. Children and age-related privacy

La Ficha is a general-purpose dominoes scorekeeping application. Advertising and consent settings may differ for users subject to children's privacy or age-of-consent rules. Before the iOS app is released, we will review the intended age rating and configure relevant age-related advertising restrictions. Parents or guardians with concerns about a minor's privacy can contact us using the email address below.

8. Security and international processing

We design La Ficha's core scorekeeping and image recognition to run locally to limit unnecessary transmission of game data. Information processed by Google or advertising partners may be handled in countries outside your location, subject to their privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards. No internet-based service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as La Ficha's functionality, advertising integration, or privacy requirements change. We will update the effective date when material changes are made. The final iOS release will be checked against this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before publication.
